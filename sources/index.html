Times of The World — Sources

Our sources, in the open

Every source the newsroom uses carries a declared reliability tier: T1 wire services, official bodies and primary data; T2 major outlets; T3 specialist and regional sources; T4 aggregators, commentary and raw leads, which never verify a story on their own. A claim is corroborated by distinct publisher families — syndicated copies of one publisher count once. State-affiliated sources are tagged, not hidden: they are evidence for their own government’s position, never independent confirmation of it. A source with no declared tier here cannot corroborate anything we publish.

Duration is the story. A short waiver is crisis management; a license through April 2027 is a de facto sanctions regime change for Russian refined products. US sanctions on Russian energy have been the West's central economic weapon since 2022. A long-dated license punches a hole in that regime — and hands Moscow leverage: every renewal date becomes a negotiation.

Competing interpretations

Reading one: pragmatic — markets need certainty, and a long license prevents a price cliff. Reading two: this is normalization by the back door, executed without a public policy debate. Reading three: the date may be a ceiling, not a commitment — licenses can be revoked.

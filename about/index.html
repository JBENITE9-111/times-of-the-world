Times of The World — About

About Times of The World

Times of The World is an independent digital newspaper publishing verified geopolitical analysis. Every story ships as a two-part package: a structured evidence dossier (what happened, what is verified, what is contested, sources) and a full analytical paper of at least 4,000 words.

Editorial standards

  • Consequential claims require one primary plus one independent source, or two genuinely independent sources. Single-source claims stay attributed.
  • Corrections are published visibly on the story page with a timestamp.
  • Photography is source-supplied, official, public-domain, or properly licensed — always captioned and credited. Event photography is never fabricated.
  • Every source carries a declared reliability tier, published in full: our source roster. Corroboration counts distinct publisher families — syndicated copies of one publisher count once.
  • Opinion is labeled as opinion. Analysis is labeled as analysis.

The newsroom

Times of The World is published by an independent editorial team producing original reporting and analysis daily. Stories are reviewed against a standing verification checklist before publication — 10/10 or it does not ship.

Contact & corrections

News tips, corrections, and licensing inquiries: YouTube — @TimesWorldNews. Corrections are actioned within 24 hours and noted on the story.

Every correction and verification-label change is published on our corrections log.
